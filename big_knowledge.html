The Intelligence Gap: Validating the Cycle of Tech Adoption

From Data Lakes to Vector Databases: Why storage capabilities historically and currently outpace actionable business intelligence.

The proposition that Big Data technologies initially outpaced Analytics—and that a parallel phenomenon is occurring today with LLMs and Agentic AI—is entirely validated by historical enterprise architecture patterns.

📂 The Big Data Era

Organizations stockpiled massive volumes of unstructured data (Hadoop), but lacked the refined analytical tools to translate it into actionable insights.

🤖 The Knowledge Era

Today, we can rapidly ingest and retrieve vast organizational knowledge using Vector DBs and RAG. However, Agentic AI for autonomous execution is still lagging.

Where Agentic AI is Lagging

1

Reliability & Hallucination

While RAG grounds single queries, chained reasoning by agents often derails over long horizons. Brittle logic leads to catastrophic drift in multi-step workflows.

2

Tool Integration Standardization

Agents struggle with brittle APIs and non-standardised environments to execute actions. We lack the "SQL-equivalent" for agentic tool use and execution.

3

State Management

Maintaining context and state across complex, days-long business workflows is currently beyond out-of-the-box LLM capabilities.
